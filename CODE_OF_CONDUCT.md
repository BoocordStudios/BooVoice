# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We want participation in BooVoice to be safe, respectful, and productive.
Contributors are expected to:

- Be welcoming and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Give actionable feedback and accept constructive criticism in good faith.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private data.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily ban,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates these standards. Report
conduct concerns privately to a repository owner through their GitHub profile;
do not expose personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as
confidentially and promptly as practical.
